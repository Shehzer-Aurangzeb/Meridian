Trend Continuation Pullback

Directional

In a confirmed trend, a shallow pullback to the 20-period EMA is not a reversal — it is the market resting. Re-entry on that exhale is among the highest-quality setups in the book.

Directional

No. 04

Range Fade at Extreme

Contrarian

In an established range with no breakout catalysts, the edges are statistical magnets in reverse. Fade them with tight invalidation and modest targets — this is a high-frequency, low-conviction trade.
